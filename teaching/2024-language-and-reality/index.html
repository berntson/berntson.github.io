Language and Reality

Spring 2024 · Uppsala University

Daniel Berntson · daniel.berntson@filosofi.uu.se

Description

Metaphysics aims to answer the question of what reality is like. This quickly leads to questions about how language works, and whether the structure of language might in some sense reflect the structure of reality. In this course, then, we will consider a number of questions related to this project. For example: Do basic metaphysical categories reflect the basic linguistic categories? And if so which ones? What does it mean to be a realist? Is there such a thing as naturalness and why should it be the aim of philosophical inquiry? And are there radically different languages — you might call them alien languages — that better reflect the structure of reality?

Assessment

Assignments for the course include three short papers and a long final paper. The short papers will be graded pass/fail. In order to pass the course, you must receive a passing grade on all four papers. Short papers should be about 600 words. The long paper should be about 3000 words for undergraduate students and 4000 words for graduate students.

Policies

Participation

You are not required to attend lectures or participate in discussions, and so failing to attend lectures will not hurt your grade. But strong contributions to our conversations in class may improve your grade.

There is no formal distinction between lectures and seminars. All sessions will be fully interactive. So come prepared with questions, objections, ideas, and arguments!

Plagiarism

Your work must be your own, and it is your responsibility as a student to know what counts as plagiarism. For more information, see https://www.uu.se/en/students/your-rights/examination/cheating-and-plagiarism.
